BACKGROUND WORK
------------------------------------------------------------

CLASS LowStockWorker : Worker
- inventoryDao : InventoryDao
- notificationsRepository : NotificationsRepository
+ doWork() : Result
  • Checks zero-stock items
  • Loads saved phone number
  • Sends SMS alerts if enabled

------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------
• All SMS sending flows through NotificationsRepository.
• Phone number is stored in SharedPreferences ("sms_prefs").
• Auto-formatting, validation, and persistent checkmark handled in NotificationsActivity.
• WorkManager ensures background SMS alerts even when app is closed.
• MVVM ensures clean separation of concerns and testable architecture.
